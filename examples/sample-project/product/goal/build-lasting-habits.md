# Build Lasting Habits

Help people turn an intention into a habit that survives past the first few weeks, by making daily check-ins effortless and momentum visible.

## What this means

Success is measured by sustained engagement, not feature count: a tracker who is still logging the same habit eight weeks in is the outcome the product optimizes for. Every feature is judged against whether it lowers the friction of a daily check-in or strengthens the sense of momentum.
