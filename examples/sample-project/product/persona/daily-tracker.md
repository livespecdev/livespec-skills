# Daily Tracker

Person who is trying to build one or more personal habits and checks in most days to mark what they did. Works mostly on a phone, often with poor or no connectivity (commute, gym, outdoors).

## Context

The daily tracker has bounced between notes apps, calendar reminders, and paper. Their dominant frustration is the one captured by [[streaks-break-silently]]: they lose momentum the moment the tool gets in the way. They value a check-in that takes one tap and works even when the network does not.

## What success looks like

- Log a completion in under two seconds, even offline.
- See an honest streak count they can trust.
- Never lose a logged completion to a sync failure.
