# Streaks Break Silently

Trackers lose a habit streak without noticing, usually because the tool failed quietly: a check-in did not save, a reminder never fired, or a sync conflict dropped a day. The break is discovered later, when motivation is already gone.

## Why it matters

The streak is the single strongest motivator in habit tracking. A streak that breaks for a tooling reason (not a real miss) feels unfair and is the most common reason trackers abandon the app. Protecting streak integrity is therefore a product priority, not a polish item.
